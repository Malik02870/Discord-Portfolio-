Ask us on Discord
What does VOLTERRA specialize in?

Discord server development and professional community setup: channels, roles, permissions, existing bot configuration, ticket systems, verification, moderation, logging, and custom embeds.

What is the difference between the packages?

The Normal Package covers the core structure and setup. The Professional Package includes a more detailed configuration with ticket systems, verification, moderation, logging, and custom embeds. We agree the exact scope together on Discord.

Can you customize an existing server?

Yes. We can discuss reorganizing your channels, refining roles and permissions, or configuring your existing bots. Share your current setup and what you want to change on Discord.

How much will my project cost?

Both packages use a custom quote. The quote depends on your requirements and the agreed scope; no fixed price or delivery guarantee is assumed.

Do you build custom-coded bots?

The services shown here cover configuration of existing Discord bots. Custom-coded bot development is not included in the listed packages.

How do I get started?

Join our Discord server, describe your community and the features you need, and request a custom quote. We will discuss your requirements before agreeing on the work.
